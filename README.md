# Rodah Ogbonolorun | Personal Website

## About This Project

This is my personal website Rodah Ogbonolorun, Ii am Commercial Real Estate Underwriter and Virtual Assistant based in Ilorin, Kwara State, Nigeria. The site works as an online business card and portfolio. It introduces me, explains the services I  offered, shows the skills and tools used every day, and gives potential clients an easy way to get in touch with me.

The website is aimed at investors, real estate firms, law firms and business owners in the United States and elsewhere who need dependable remote support with deal analysis or day-to-day administration.

## Who It Is For

Visitors are mainly clients and employers who want to learn quickly what I do and whether I am the right fit. A visitor should be able to understand my specialty in seconds, see proof of my experience, and reach out to me without searching for my contact details.

## What the Website Contains

The site is a single page divided into six sections, each reachable from the navigation bar at the top.

**Home.** 
The first impression. It shows my name, my professional title, a short statement about the value I bring and buttons that lead to my contact section, my services, my online portfolio and my  professional networking profile. Below that are four quick highlights: more than five years of remote experience, a 108-unit build-to-rent project underwritten, more than eight U.S. markets analysed, and a team of six previously led.

**About.** A short personal introduction explaining my  background in commercial real estate underwriting, my  approach to turning raw deal data into clear investment summaries, and my  experience leading a cross-functional team. Alongside it is a card listing my  education and my  Executive Virtual Assistant certification.

**Services.** The work I offer, grouped into five areas: commercial real estate underwriting, financial modelling, market research and due diligence, executive virtual assistance, and deal pipeline and CRM management. Each area lists the specific tasks included.
