About Me

Need someone who can analyze the numbers, uncover the risks, and take work off your plate? I help investors and businesses turn complex data into clear decisions and get critical tasks done accurately and on time. I’m a Commercial Real Estate Underwriter, Financial Modeling Specialist, Researcher, and Virtual Assistant with 5+ years of experience, 200+ CRE deals underwritten, and experience supporting multifamily, commercial real estate, finance, and business operations. My strength is combining analytical thinking, attention to detail, research, Excel, and reliable administrative support to help clients make better investment decisions and keep their operations organized.
COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE UNDERWRITING
I help investors, sponsors, developers, acquisition teams, and lenders evaluate potential deals and understand the numbers behind an investment. My experience includes: • Multifamily & commercial acquisition underwriting • Value-add & repositioning analysis • Ground-up development underwriting • T12 & historical financial analysis • Rent roll & lease analysis • Revenue, expense & NOI projections • Property valuation & cap rate analysis • Debt sizing & DSCR analysis • Sources & Uses • Acquisition & development budgets • Multi-year cash flow projections • IRR, Equity Multiple & Cash-on-Cash Returns • LP/GP waterfall structures • Exit valuation & disposition analysis • Sensitivity & downside analysis I've worked on approximately $1M–$40M transactions involving properties ranging from 20 to 300+ units. I don't simply populate spreadsheets. I review assumptions, identify inconsistencies, analyze risks, and stress-test the numbers so you can better understand: What is the property worth? What returns can we realistically expect? What assumptions are driving the returns? How much debt can the property support? What could go wrong? Does the deal make sense at the proposed price?
